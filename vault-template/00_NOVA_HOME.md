# Nova Home

This is the home page for your AI-agent central brain.

Use this vault to help agents:

- understand current projects;
- find the right context;
- avoid repeating old mistakes;
- preserve important work;
- verify before claiming completion;
- hand work off cleanly.

## Daily Use

1. Add ideas or raw material to the right inbox.
2. Ask the agent to route it through `PROJECT_INTAKE_ROUTER.md`.
3. Keep active projects under `10_PROJECTS/`.
4. Keep generated files outside the vault under `<RUNTIME_ROOT>`.
5. Record meaningful work in `50_TASKS/AGENT_WORK_LOG.md`.
