# Risk Gates

Stop and ask for explicit approval before:

- hard delete;
- broad file move or rename;
- broad stable-memory rewrite;
- installer, plugin, hook, or config change;
- deployment;
- authentication, account, payment, or secret/API-key handling;
- browser-account actions;
- OS repair, registry, services, drivers, boot, disk, antivirus, or whole-machine cleanup;
- autonomous recurring edits.

When stopping, report:

```text
risk:
exact file or command:
why it matters:
safer option:
backup or rollback:
verification:
```
